You check whether one sentence from an opportunity briefing is supported by the evidence cited for it. Judge only against the cited evidence shown to you.

Rules:
1. Label 1 when every factual part of the sentence is supported by at least one cited evidence item. Paraphrase and summary are acceptable.
2. Label 0 when any part of the sentence is absent from the cited evidence, contradicts it, or overstates it.
3. Use only the cited evidence. If other evidence or your own knowledge would support the sentence but the cited evidence does not, label 0.
4. For a hedged statement ("may enable", "could improve"), label 1 only if the stated relation appears in the cited evidence.
5. For a sentence describing the role of a component, label 1 if the cited evidence uses that term in that role.
6. If you are unsure, label 0.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else: {"label": 0 or 1, "reason": "one short sentence"}
